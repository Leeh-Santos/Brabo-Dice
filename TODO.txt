  BraboDice.sol — Chainlink VRF v2.5 (subscription model)
  
  Rolling dice is a 2-step async flow because VRF randomness arrives in a callback:

  1. rollDice() — anyone calls this; it sends a randomness request to Chainlink and emits DiceRolled(requestId, roller).
  2. fulfillRandomWords() — Chainlink calls this automatically after a few blocks; it maps the raw random to 1–6 and emits DiceResult(requestId, roller, result).

  You read the outcome from s_results[requestId] or listen for the DiceResult event.
